STEP 10 — ATTENDANCE ANALYTICS & REPORTS

New page: /reports.html

Features:
- Filter by date range, course, branch, semester, section, and subject.
- Subject-level attendance percentage and eligible student-session totals.
- Students below 75% attendance list.
- CSV export including expected slots with missing records represented as ABSENT.
- Teacher analytics and exports are restricted to the teacher's assigned timetable entries at the backend.

API endpoints:
- GET /api/reports/analytics
- GET /api/reports/export.csv

Run from the directory containing pom.xml: mvn spring-boot:run
Open http://localhost:8081/reports.html after staff sign-in.

Testing note: ZIP integrity and inline JavaScript syntax should be checked; Maven/live database build may not be available in this environment. Review analytics against institutional attendance policy before official use.
